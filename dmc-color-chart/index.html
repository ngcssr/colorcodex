DMC color chart FAQ

What is a DMC color chart and how do I use it?

A DMC color chart lists every DMC embroidery thread with its number, color name, and Hex/RGB approximation. Use it to match threads by color before starting a project, or to convert design swatches into DMC numbers.

Is this DMC color guide complete?

This DMC color guide covers 50+ high-frequency shades spanning reds, browns, greens, blues, and neutrals — the colors used in most cross-stitch and embroidery patterns. For the full 487-shade line, consult the DMC official color card.

How do I identify DMC thread colors from an existing image?

Upload your image to our image color picker, click a pixel, and read the returned Hex — then match it against the closest DMC thread colors row in the table below.

Does the DMC floss chart include Anchor and conversion numbers?

The DMC floss chart lists DMC numbers only. For a DMC to anchor conversion, match the DMC Hex to the closest Anchor Hex and note both numbers — Anchor shades that are perceptually similar will share approximate Hex values.

Where can I find the DMC color numbers for a specific shade?

Use the table below — sort by DMC number or scan by color family. Each row shows the DMC color numbers, the official DMC name, and the approximate Hex/RGB reference.

Can I convert DMC to anchor conversion chart values from this page?

Yes — the DMC to anchor conversion logic is straightforward: find your DMC Hex, then compare it to an Anchor chart's Hex values, picking the perceptually closest match. Anchor numbers are usually listed alongside DMC in conversion charts.
